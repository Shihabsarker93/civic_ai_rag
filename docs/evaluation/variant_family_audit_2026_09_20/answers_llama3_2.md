Model: llama3.2; route: llm; time: 56.39 s; expected evidence present: True.

Answer:

According to Legal Unit 20, an online application can be submitted within 45 days of birth or death in certain cases:

(1) A healthcare provider or a health worker can submit the information about a person's birth or death to the registrar at their designated place of work within 45 days.

(2) A non-Bangladeshi citizen residing abroad (excluding those born outside Bangladesh) can submit the information about another person's birth or death to the registrar at their permanent address through an online application within 45 days.

(3) In accordance with government decisions, a Bangladeshi citizen living abroad who was not born in Bangladesh but has a valid passport can register a birth within 45 days.

(4) After submitting an online application, a computer-generated number will be issued along with a copy of the application form, and necessary documents and certificates will be attached thereto. The attached documents should then be filed with the registrar at the workplace of the applicant or his/her guardian.

Note that these procedures may vary depending on specific circumstances as stated under sub-section (5).
