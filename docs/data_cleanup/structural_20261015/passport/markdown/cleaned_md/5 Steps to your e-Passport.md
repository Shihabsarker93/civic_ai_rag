# 5 Steps to your e-Passport

## Overview
* **Last updated:** 3 June 2026
* **Description:** You can apply for the new e-Passport in 5 easy steps

## Application Steps

### Step 1: Check if the new e-Passport is already available in your area
* **Action/Resource:** List of functional e-Passport Offices

### Step 2: Fill in your e-Passport application online
* **Action/Resource:** For Online Application Click Here

### Step 3: Pay passport fees
* **Action/Resource:** For Passport Fees and Bank List Click Here

### Step 4: Visit your Passport Office for biometric enrolment
* **Requirement:** Make sure you have all required documents with you when you visit the passport office.

### Step 5: Collect your e-Passport at the passport office
* **Collection Requirement 1:** Delivery slip you received during passport enrolment
* **Collection Requirement 2:** Authorized representatives (has to bring his/her NID card) can collect the applicant's new passport.
