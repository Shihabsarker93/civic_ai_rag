### Note Verbale

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Government of the People's Republic of Bangladesh presents its compliments to the Royal Embassy of Saudi Arabia in Dhaka and has the honour to inform that Ministry of Road Transport and Bridges of Bangladesh Government has informed that due to exhaustion of reserved stock of personalized Smart Driving Cards upon excessive demands and delivery, the Royal Embassy of the Kingdom Saudi Arabia in Dhaka could kindly accept Acknowledgement Slips from Bangladeshi motor vehicle drivers when they apply for Saudi visas in lieu of Driving License Smart Cards and Printed Driving License Cards which are normally issued by Bangladesh Road Transport Authority.

Therefore, the Royal Embassy of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia is requested to accept Acknowledgement Slips from the Bangladeshi applicants of driving visas wishing to go to the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia in lieu of personalized Smart Driving Cards.

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Government of the People's Republic of Bangladesh avails itself of this opportunity to renew to the Royal Embassy of Saudi Arabia in Dhaka the assurances of its highest consideration.
