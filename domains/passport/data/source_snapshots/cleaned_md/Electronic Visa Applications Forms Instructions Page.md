# Electronic Visa Applications Forms Instructions Page[cite: 16]

## What You Need[cite: 16]
Welcome to the Electronic Visa Applications Forms Instructions Page. Forms available on this page can be filled out on-line and assist in the processing of your application[cite: 16].

### Technical System Requirements[cite: 16]
* **Internet Explorer (Windows):** The minimum version that will work with this site is version 9.0[cite: 16].
* **Mozilla Firefox:** The minimum version that will work with this site is version 3.5[cite: 16].
* **Adobe Acrobat Reader:** You must also have Adobe Acrobat Reader version 8.0 or higher installed on your PC in order to download and print the completed application form[cite: 16].
* **Printer:** You should use either an ink-jet or laser printer to print the completed application form[cite: 16].

### Supported Services and Applications[cite: 16]
* **Supported Countries Check:** Before you apply, please check our supported countries where you can find out if the country you are applying accepts on-line applications[cite: 16].
* **Allowed Visa Services:** Currently the service allows submission of applications for NEW VISA, EXTENSION OF VISA, NO VISA REQUIRED (NVR) and ON ARRIVAL VISA (OAV)[cite: 16].

### Required Information and Preparations[cite: 16]
* **Email Address:** You will require an Email-address to complete the online forms. This is for security reasons and also so that we can contact you regarding your online application[cite: 16].
* **Printing Capability:** We will also ask you to print your online visa application form as part of this process; therefore you will need the ability to print from your computer[cite: 16].
* **Information to Have on Hand:** We also suggest that you have the following information on hand[cite: 16]:
    * **Passport:**[cite: 16]
    * **Digital Photo:** 45mm x 35mm Digital Photo in JPEG format; Maximum size is 300kb (Photo is optional)[cite: 16].
    * **Address in Bangladesh:**[cite: 16]
    * **Email Address:**[cite: 16]
* **Saving Progress:** You can save your Visa application on any page where you see this[cite: 16].

### Online Application Process Steps[cite: 16]
The online application process requires the user to[cite: 16]:
* **Step 1:** Fill up and submit the Online Visa Application Form[cite: 16]
* **Step 2:** Print the completed form[cite: 16]
* **Step 3:** Take the printed form enclosed with required documents, payment of visa fees documents to the nearest Visa office / Bangladesh Mission[cite: 16]

### Additional Information and Policies[cite: 16]
* **Visa Fees:** Current visa fees are available Here[cite: 16].
* **Terms & Conditions:** For details on our data protection and copyright obligations, please refer to our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy[cite: 16].
* **Guidance Acknowledgment:** Ensure that you have read these and all the relevant guidance notes and confirm this by ticking the checkbox below[cite: 16].

### Legal Warnings and Penalties[cite: 16]
* **Application Refusal & Ban:** Your application may be refused and you may be banned from coming to Bangladesh for several years if you use a false document, lie or withhold relevant information[cite: 16]. 
* **Immigration Law Breach:** You may also be banned if you have breached immigration laws in the Bangladesh[cite: 16].
* **False Travel Document Offence:** "New" travelers to the Bangladesh who produce a false travel document or passport to the Bangladesh immigration authorities for themselves and/or their children are committing an offence[cite: 16].
* **Penalties:** People found guilty of this offence face up to two years in prison or a fine (or both)[cite: 16].
