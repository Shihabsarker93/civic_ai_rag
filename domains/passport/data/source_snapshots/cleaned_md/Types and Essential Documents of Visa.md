# Types and Essential Documents of Visa

Visa is usually issued by Bangladesh High Commission/Embassy concerned and extended by Department of Immigration and Passports, Government of the People's Republic of Bangladesh.

---

## Visa Categories and Requirements

### 01. Visa Type A
* **S.L. No.:** 01
* **Type of Visa:** A
* **Who are Eligible / Arrival / Travel Purpose:** Head of State / Chief of Government / Minister / State Ministers / Deputy Ministers / Members of Parliament / Regional Council Member / Mayor / And equivalent persons and their spouse and dependent family other family members | Government / official duty
* **Essential Documents to Issue:** Note verbal
* **Essential Documents to Extend:** Note verbal

### 02. Visa Type A1
* **S.L. No.:** 02
* **Type of Visa:** A1
* **Who are Eligible / Arrival / Travel Purpose:** Government/Semi-Government / Autonomous Agency officials / government representatives and their team members and their spouse and dependent children | Government / official duty
* **Essential Documents to Issue:** Note Verbal
* **Essential Documents to Extend:** Recommendation of the concerned ministry/ organizing agency.

### 03. Visa Type A2
* **S.L. No.:** 03
* **Type of Visa:** A2
* **Who are Eligible / Arrival / Travel Purpose:** Officers and employees deployed by The United Nations and its organization/ International / Regional Agency/organizations | Official duty
* **Essential Documents to Issue:** Recommendations of organization concerned.
* **Essential Documents to Extend:** Request of Ministry/ Department/agency concerned and terms and conditions laid down in appointment.

### 04. Visa Type FA2
* **S.L. No.:** 04
* **Type of Visa:** FA2
* **Who are Eligible / Arrival / Travel Purpose:** Spouse and dependent family members of A2 | Traveling with A2
* **Essential Documents to Issue:** Papers as proof of spouse/dependent family members of A2 visa holders.
* **Essential Documents to Extend:** Request of concerned organization/agency and copy of related A2 visa.

### 05. Visa Type A3
* **S.L. No.:** 05
* **Type of Visa:** A3
* **Who are Eligible / Arrival / Travel Purpose:** Specialists / consultant/ Officer/ Employee / Worker employed in the project under bilateral/ multilateral agreement between government of the People's Republic of Bangladesh and its development partners | Professional Responsibility /Job
* **Essential Documents to Issue:** Recommendations of the concerned Government organization/Economic Relations Division.
* **Essential Documents to Extend:** Recommendations of the concerned Ministry/Economic Relations Division/government organization, security clearance and contract between government & others.

### 06. Visa Type FA3
* **S.L. No.:** 06
* **Type of Visa:** FA3
* **Who are Eligible / Arrival / Travel Purpose:** Spouse and dependent family members of A3 | Traveling with A3
* **Essential Documents to Issue:** Recommendations of the concerned Ministry/ Department/Economic Relations Division, for the same period of related A3 visa.
* **Essential Documents to Extend:** Request from concerned Ministry/department/agency and favorable police report and copy of related A3 visa.

### 07. Visa Type B
* **S.L. No.:** 07
* **Type of Visa:** B
* **Who are Eligible / Arrival / Travel Purpose:** Businessman / business representative | Business
* **Essential Documents to Issue:** Investment/business certificate issued by the recognized trade association of the concerned/ local (Bangladesh) sponsor's recommendation and document regarding the authenticity of his business.
* **Essential Documents to Extend:** Business certificate/trade license, tax certificate, recommendations of local sponsor, and favorable police report.

### 08. Visa Type C
* **S.L. No.:** 08
* **Type of Visa:** C
* **Who are Eligible / Arrival / Travel Purpose:** Crew of Aircraft / Ships / Other Transport engaged in international routes | Professional Responsibility /Job
* **Essential Documents to Issue:** Recommendation of the recruitment authority.
* **Essential Documents to Extend:** The recommendation of proper authority

### 09. Visa Type D
* **S.L. No.:** 09
* **Type of Visa:** D
* **Who are Eligible / Arrival / Travel Purpose:** Ambassador / Diplomat / Consular and equivalent officer and their spouse and dependent family members | Duties of the official duty
* **Essential Documents to Issue:** Note verbal country

### 14. Visa Type E1
* **S.L. No.:** 14
* **Type of Visa:** E1
* **Who are Eligible / Arrival / Travel Purpose:** Foreigners engaged in Machinery and software supplies / Installation / maintenance / supervision / project Inspection and such things | Machinery and software providing/Setting/ Maintenance/training/ Supervision / project inspection Etc.
* **Essential Documents to Issue:** Authenticity of BIDA/BEPZA/ department/agency/ concerned Ministry's recommendation. Invitation letter from local sponsor, Authenticity of the institution/importer who invites the foreigner, Contract between machinery/software foreign provider/supplier and local institution/importer.
* **Essential Documents to Extend:** Recommendation of local institution/importer, positive police report and work permit.

* **Essential Documents to Extend:** Security clearance; request from concerned organization/ institution and recommendation of BIDA/BEPZA declaring that investment of the relevant applicant is existing on.
