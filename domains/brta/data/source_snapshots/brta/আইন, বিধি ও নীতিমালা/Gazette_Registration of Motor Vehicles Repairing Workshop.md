### "70A. Registration of Motor Vehicles Repairing Workshop.—[cite: 18]

(1) No person or body of persons or establishment shall carry on or engage in the business of repair and maintenance of motor vehicles without registration granted by the registering authority under sub-rule (2) in the Form A.M.W.C.[cite: 18]

(2) The registering authority for registration of motor vehicle repairing workshop shall be—[cite: 18]
* **(a) Metropolitan Area:** In a metropolitan area, the Divisional Deputy Director (Engr.) of the Authority having jurisdiction over the area concerned or any other officer of the Authority authorized in this behalf by the Authority;[cite: 18]
* **(b) Other Areas:** Other than the area mentioned in clause (a), the Assistant Director (Engr.) of the Authority having jurisdiction over the area concerned or any other officer of the Authority authorized in this behalf by the Authority.[cite: 18]

(3) The registering authority may grant registration of the motor vehicle repairing workshop under the following categories:[cite: 18]
* **(a)** Motor cycle, three wheeler motor vehicles;[cite: 18]
* **(b)** Light motor vehicle;[cite: 18]
* **(c)** Medium/heavy motor vehicle; or[cite: 18]
* **(d)** Any other vehicle.[cite: 18]

(4) No motor vehicle repairing workshop shall be granted registration unless recommended by the Selection Board consist of the following members, namely :—[cite: 18]
* **(a) Chairman:** The Chairman, Bangladesh Road Transport Authority (BRTA), who shall also be the Chairman of the Selection Board;[cite: 18]
* **(b) Member:** An officer, not below the rank of Deputy Secretary, of Roads & Railway Division, Ministry of Communication to be nominated by the Secretary of that Division;[cite: 18]
* **(c) Member:** An officer of the Government Transport Directorate to be nominated by the Commissioner of that Directorate;[cite: 18]
* **(d) Member:** An officer of BITAC to be nominated by Director General, BITAC;[cite: 18]
* **(e) Member-Secretary:** The Director (Operation) of BRTA, who shall also be the Member-Secretary of the Selection Board.[cite: 18]

(5) Before making recommendation for registration, the Selection Board shall satisfy itself—[cite: 18]
* **(a)** that the applicant and the persons employed are of good character;[cite: 18]
* **(b)** that the persons employed to repair the vehicles have relevant technical knowledge in automobiles;[cite: 18]
* **(c)** that the applicant is financially sound;[cite: 18]
* **(d)** that the proprietor maintains an adequate space to accommodate at least 5 vehicles of the category for which he has applied for;[cite: 18]
* **(e)** that the proprietor has valid trade license, Tax Identification Number (TIN) and other necessary documents;[cite: 18]
* **(f)** that the applicant maintains a record of history with vehicle registration number repaired from time to time, name and identification of repaired or replaced parts, the dates etc;[cite: 18]
* **(g)** that the applicant maintains suitable tools, equipment and other necessary instruments for repair and maintenance motor vehicles;[cite: 18]
* **(h)** that the age of applicant is not below 20 years at the time of application.[cite: 18]

(6) The registering authority may refuse to grant registration of a motor vehicle repairing workshop, if the conditions under sub-rule (5) are not fulfilled.[cite: 18]

(7) The registering authority may by an order from time to time—[cite: 18]
* **(a)** determine procedure for establishment, operation and control of motor vehicle repairing workshop;[cite: 18]
* **(b)** determine terms and condition of motor vehicle repairing workshop;[cite: 18]
* **(c)** classify motor vehicle repairing workshop for imparting repair and maintenance of light or heavy or other type of motor vehicle;[cite: 18]

(8) Any change in the address of the place of business of a motor vehicle repairing workshop shall, within fifteen days of such change, be informed in writing to the registering authority.[cite: 18]

(9) Every motor vehicle repairing workshop, its records, equipment, tools and machinery shall be opened at all reasonable times for inspection by the Director (Operation) of Bangladesh Road Transport Authority or any other officer authorized by him in this behalf or the registering authority of that workshop or any other officer authorized by him.[cite: 18]

(10) The registering authority for the registration of an motor vehicle repairing workshop may, at any-time, for reasons to be intimated in writing, withdraw the registration and thereupon the holder thereof shall surrender the certificate (Form AMWC) issued to him within seven days from the date of such intimation.[cite: 18]

(11) The registering authority, in consultation with the Bangladesh Road Transport Authority, from time to time, may determine the rate of charge to be paid by the owner of the vehicle or the representative thereof, for the purpose of repairing or maintenance etc. of the Motor Vehicle.[cite: 18]

(12) Any person aggrieved by an order of refusal or withdrawal of registration made by the registering authority may, within thirty days of the communication of such order, appeal to the Secretary, Roads and Railway Division of the Ministry of Communication.[cite: 18]

(13) The procedure laid down in rule 88 for the conduct and hearing of appeal shall also apply to all appeals preferred under sub-rule (12) of this rule.[cite: 18]

### 70B. Procedure for registration of motor vehicle repairing workshop.—[cite: 18]

(1) Any person who desires to establish a motor vehicle repairing workshop to carry on or engage in the business of repair and maintenance of particular category of motor vehicles shall apply in the Form E.M.W.A with necessary papers and documents to the registering authority in whose functional area he/she wishes to establish the motor vehicle repairing workshop.[cite: 18]

(2) The registering authority shall then endorse the application to the concerned Inspector of Motor Vehicles. The concerned Inspector of Motor Vehicles shall physically inspect the motor vehicle repairing workshop and shall satisfy himself that the requirements of the rules are complied with and shall forward the application to the registering authority with his comments.[cite: 18]

(3) The registering authority shall place the application before the Selection Board constituted under sub-rule (4) of rule 70A for recommendation and if the Selection Board recommends for such registration, the registration fee of a motor vehicle repairing workshop shall be deposited in a prescribed manner determined by the registering authority.[cite: 18]

(4) Subject to sub-rule (3) the registering authority shall grant registration.[cite: 18]

(5) The Government may, from time to time, by an order, determine the fee for the registration of a motor vehicle repairing workshop.[cite: 18]

(6) The registration of motor vehicle repairing workshop under this rule shall remain valid for one year and may be renewed thereafter.[cite: 18]

---

### 70C. Addition or Alteration of category to the certificate of registration of motor vehicle repairing workshop.—[cite: 18]

(1) Any person or body of persons or establishment holding a certificate of registration of motor vehicle repairing workshop under sub-rule (1) of rule 70A may apply in Form A.E.M.W.A for the addition or alteration of category of motor vehicles for repair and maintenance.[cite: 18]

(2) The procedure for addition or alteration of other category of motor vehicles to the certificate of registration of motor vehicle repairing workshop shall be the same as of rule 70B.[cite: 18]

(3) A fee equal to half of the fee provided under sub-rule (4) of rule 70B shall be charged for addition to or alteration of a certificate of registration of motor vehicle repairing workshop.[cite: 18]

(4) A person, holding a certificate of registration of motor vehicle repairing workshop for heavy motor vehicles does not require registration for medium motor vehicles, light motor vehicles, three wheeler vehicles and motor cycle.[cite: 18]

---

### 70D. Renewal of the certificate of registration of motor vehicle repairing workshop.—[cite: 18]

(1) An application for the renewal of a certificate of registration of motor vehicle repairing workshop shall be made in Form R.M.W.C to the registering authority of that workshop along with original certificate of registration.[cite: 18]

(2) The Government may, from time to time, by an order, determine the fee for the yearly renewal fee of a certificate of registration of motor vehicle repairing workshop.[cite: 18]

(3) Before renewing the certificate of registration of motor vehicle repairing workshop, the registering authority shall satisfy that the workshop fulfills the conditions under of sub-rule (5) of rules 70A.[cite: 18]

(4) The registering authority may renew the certificate of registration of motor vehicle repairing workshop for three years, but not less than one year, at a time from the date of expiry.[cite: 18]

(5) The application for the renewal of a certificate of registration of motor vehicle repairing workshop must be submitted to the registering authority before the last date of validity of the certificate, otherwise, additional taka ten will be charged for every one day.[cite: 18]

---

### 70E. Issue of duplicate certificate of registration.—[cite: 18]

(1) If a certificate of registration of motor vehicle repairing workshop is lost by the holder or is destroyed, the holder shall apply in a letter for the duplicate certificate of registration to the registering authority of the workshop describing the particulars of the certificate of registration. In case of lost of certificate of registration, the applicant shall attach a copy of general diary filed in the police station along with the application.[cite: 18]

(2) The fee for a duplicate certificate registration under this rule shall be taka one hundred.[cite: 18]

(3) If the certificate of registration is lost, it is necessary for the holder to inform the matter to the nearest police station and make a general diary there.[cite: 18]

(4) If the application is properly made and the requisite fee is deposited, the registering authority of the workshop shall issue a duplicate certificate of registration.[cite: 18]

(5) When a duplicate certificate of registration is issued, it shall be clearly stamped "Duplicate" in red and shall be marked with the date of issue of the duplicate and the seal of the registering authority;[cite: 18]
